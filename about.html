About La French Tech Denver

We are a curated support community helping entrepreneurs thrive through a strong network and shared best practices.

Our Mission

Bring French entrepreneurs together, help develop startups, and support French-speaking tech players in the Denver/Boulder region. We connect French startups with investors and local tech leaders.

Goals

  • Support existing French startups in Colorado
  • Grow an ecosystem where anyone can build in the Rockies
  • Bridge the local tech scene with French founders and talent

Our Values

  • Collaboration — Support & Accelerate
  • Innovation — Empower & Federate
  • Entrepreneurship — Inspire & Shine
